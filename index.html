FAQ

Is SellersPoint really free to start?

Yes - the Starter plan is free forever, with 30 orders and 5 products a month. No card required to sign up.

Do I need a website or technical skills?

No. Everything works from your phone's browser, and your free storefront link is ready as soon as you upgrade to a paid plan.

How do customers pay me?

Orders placed through your storefront come to you via WhatsApp with your bank details attached - customers pay you directly, no processing fees.

Can I track more than one product or service?

Yes - add products, services, or digital items, each with their own price, stock, photos, and optional discount.

What happens if I outgrow the free plan?

Upgrade any time from Settings - your data carries over, nothing is lost, and you can downgrade again later.

Can I migrate from Excel?

Yes - import your product and customer lists as a CSV file exported from Excel or Google Sheets, right from the Products and Customers tabs.

Can I import customers?

Yes - the same CSV import works for customers (name, phone, email, location) so you're not typing them in one by one.

Does SellersPoint work offline?

No - it's a web app that needs an internet connection, which is what keeps your data synced and accessible from any device.

Can I use multiple staff?

Yes - Pro and Business plans include staff seats so your team can help manage orders and customers.

How secure is my data?

Your data is hosted on Supabase (Postgres) over encrypted connections. Card payments are handled directly by Paystack - we never see or store your card details.
