Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 July 2026

This is a working draft, not reviewed by a lawyer. Have it checked against the Nigeria Data Protection Act before relying on it.

This policy explains what data SellersPoint collects, why, and how it's handled.

1. What we collect

When you sign up: your email, business name, and phone number. As you use the app: the products, customers, orders, and business profile details (name, phone, address, logo) you enter. Customer records you add (name, phone, location) are your business's data, entered by you.

2. Who processes your data

We use two subprocessors: Supabase for authentication and database hosting, and Paystack for payment processing. We don't store your card details - Paystack handles those directly. We don't sell your data to anyone.

3. Why we collect it

To operate your account, enforce plan limits, process payments, generate invoices/receipts, and provide AI-assisted tools. Anonymous use of the free Receipt Generator doesn't create an account or attach data to one.

4. Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, contact us and we'll remove your business data within a reasonable period, except where we're required to keep payment records for accounting/legal purposes.

5. Your rights

You can access, correct, or request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us. If you're in Nigeria, you have rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023.

6. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's session storage to keep you signed in (via Supabase Auth) - no third-party advertising trackers.

7. Changes to this policy

We'll update the "Last updated" date above if this policy changes materially.

8. Contact

Questions or data requests: privacy@sellerspoint.app.
