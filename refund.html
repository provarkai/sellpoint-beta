Refund Policy

Last updated: 8 July 2026

1. Subscription plans (Growth, Pro, Business, Enterprise)

Plans activate immediately on successful payment via Paystack. You can request a full refund within 48 hours of a new subscription or upgrade if you haven't meaningfully used the plan's paid features (e.g., inviting staff, using extra AI generations, adding a branch). After 48 hours, or once paid features have been used, payments are non-refundable.

2. Cancelling a subscription

You can downgrade or cancel from your account settings at any time. Your current plan stays active until the end of the billing period you've already paid for - we don't pro-rate partial refunds for unused days.

3. Add-ons (extra AI credits, staff seats, branches)

Add-on purchases are one-off and non-refundable once granted, since the extra AI credits, seat, or branch is applied to your account immediately on payment.

4. Failed or duplicate charges

If you're charged more than once for the same upgrade, or charged without your plan activating, contact us with your payment reference and we'll investigate and refund the error promptly.

5. How to request a refund

Email support@sellerspoint.ng with your account email and Paystack payment reference. We aim to respond within 2 business days.

6. The free Receipt Generator

The Receipt Generator is free and doesn't involve any payment to us, so this policy doesn't apply to it.
